5 Things Every Beginner Crocheter Needs

Starting crochet doesn't have to be complicated. Here are five useful things to have when you're beginning.

1. A Crochet Hook

Choose a hook that is comfortable to hold and suitable for the yarn you're using.

2. Yarn

Smooth yarn can make it easier for beginners to see their stitches while practicing.

3. Scissors

You'll need scissors for cutting yarn when finishing a project.

4. A Yarn Needle

A yarn needle helps you weave in loose ends and finish your projects neatly.

5. Patience

Crochet takes practice. Don't worry if your first stitches aren't perfect. Keep practicing and enjoy learning.
