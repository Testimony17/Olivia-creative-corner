Double Crochet Guide

Learn how to make the double crochet stitch step by step.

What You Need

  • Crochet hook
  • Yarn
  • Scissors

How to Make a Double Crochet

  1. Make a foundation chain.
  2. Yarn over your hook.
  3. Insert the hook into the stitch.
  4. Yarn over and pull up a loop.
  5. Yarn over and pull through two loops.
  6. Yarn over again and pull through the remaining two loops.

Practice

Practice the stitch slowly until your stitches become even and comfortable.
