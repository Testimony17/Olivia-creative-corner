How I Started Crocheting

Crochet became one of the creative activities that allowed me to turn simple materials into something I could be proud of.

I started by learning the basics and practicing simple stitches. At first, some stitches were difficult, but practicing helped me become more comfortable with the hook and yarn.

As I improved, I began experimenting with different projects and discovering how much creativity can go into crochet.

That journey eventually inspired me to create Olivia Creative Corner, where I can share what I make and encourage other people to explore their own creativity.
