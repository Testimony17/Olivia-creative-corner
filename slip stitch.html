Slip Stitch Guide

Learn how to make a slip stitch and use it to join crochet pieces or move your hook across a row.

What You Need

  • Crochet hook
  • Yarn
  • Scissors

How to Make a Slip Stitch

  1. Insert your hook into the stitch.
  2. Yarn over your hook.
  3. Pull the yarn through the stitch and the loop already on your hook.
  4. Continue as directed by your pattern.

Practice

Practice making several slip stitches until you can make them smoothly without pulling the yarn too tightly.
