Crochet Supplies Guide

Starting crochet doesn't require many supplies. Here are some basic tools you can use to begin your crochet journey.

Essential Supplies

  • Crochet Hook: Choose a hook that works well with your yarn.
  • Yarn: Beginners can start with a smooth, medium-weight yarn that is easy to see and handle.
  • Scissors: Use scissors to cut your yarn when finishing a project.
  • Yarn Needle: A yarn needle helps you weave loose ends into your finished project.
  • Stitch Markers: These can help you keep track of specific stitches or rounds.

Choosing Your First Yarn

Choose yarn that is smooth and not too difficult to see. A lighter or medium shade can also make your stitches easier to identify while learning.

Beginner Tip

You don't need to buy everything at once. Start with a crochet hook, some yarn, scissors, and a yarn needle. You can add other tools as you gain experience.
