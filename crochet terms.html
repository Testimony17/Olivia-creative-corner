Crochet Terms Guide

Crochet patterns often use abbreviations and special terms. Learning these terms can make patterns much easier to understand.

Common Crochet Terms

  • Chain (ch): A basic crochet stitch commonly used to create a foundation chain.
  • Slip Stitch (sl st): A short stitch often used to join stitches or move across a row.
  • Single Crochet (sc): A basic stitch that creates a relatively short and firm fabric.
  • Half Double Crochet (hdc): A stitch that is taller than a single crochet.
  • Double Crochet (dc): A taller basic crochet stitch.
  • Yarn Over (yo): Placing the yarn over the crochet hook to create a loop.
  • Round: A circular row of crochet stitches.
  • Row: A horizontal line of crochet stitches.
  • Stitch (st): An individual crochet loop or completed stitch.

Why Learn Crochet Terms?

Understanding common crochet terms makes it easier to read patterns, follow tutorials, and communicate with other crocheters.
