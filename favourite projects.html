My Favourite Crochet Projects

A collection of things I've created with crochet.

One of my favorite things about crochet is being able to turn yarn into something useful, beautiful, and completely my own. Over time, I've made many different projects, and each one has given me an opportunity to learn and experiment.

My Projects

School Bag

Making a crochet school bag allowed me to create something practical while exploring different stitches and designs.

Hats and Caps

Hats and caps are fun projects because I can experiment with different colors, textures, and styles.

Beanie

Making a beanie helped me practice working in a rounded shape and creating a comfortable finished piece.

Rose Flowers

Crochet roses are one of my favorite decorative projects. They are small, creative, and can be used in different ways.

Scrunchies

Crochet scrunchies are simple and fun projects that can add a creative touch to an everyday accessory.

Clothes

Creating crochet clothes has allowed me to experiment with larger projects and different patterns.

Granny Squares

Granny squares are especially interesting because individual squares can be combined to create larger projects such as bags, clothing, and blankets.

Why I Love Making These Projects

Each project is different. Some are practical, some are decorative, and others simply give me a chance to experiment. Crochet has taught me to be patient, creative, and willing to keep trying when something doesn't work the first time.
