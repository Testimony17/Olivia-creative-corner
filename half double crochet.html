Half Double Crochet Guide

The half double crochet is a versatile stitch that is taller than a single crochet but shorter than a double crochet.

What You Need

  • Crochet hook
  • Yarn
  • Scissors

How to Make a Half Double Crochet

  1. Yarn over your hook.
  2. Insert the hook into the stitch.
  3. Yarn over and pull up a loop.
  4. You should now have three loops on your hook.
  5. Yarn over again.
  6. Pull the yarn through all three loops.

Practice

Practice several rows while keeping your tension consistent.
